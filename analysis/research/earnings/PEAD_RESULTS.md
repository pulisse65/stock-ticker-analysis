## Definitions

- Reaction session: report date if timestamp < 09:30 ET, else next session in the ticker's price index. r = close[reaction]/close[prev] - 1.
- sigma60 = std of the 60 daily close-to-close returns ending at the prior session (>=40 valid); z = r/sigma60.
- Signals: z>=2 (primary); top-quintile = pooled r >= 80th percentile of r within each set; z<=-2 (mirror, long).
- Quintile thresholds: SP100 r >= 3.98%, WATCHLIST r >= 6.68%.
- Entry = open of session after reaction session (idx0). Exit = close at idx0+H, H in 5/10/20. Net = exit/entry - 1 - 0.0010. Trades with exit past data skipped.
- rand_ret: mean of 20 random same-ticker sessions >=10 sessions from any of that ticker's events, same open/close/cost, seed 20261108. spy_ret: SPY same entry/exit dates open->close, same cost.
- t_rand / t_spy: paired t of (ret - baseline). Wilson = 95% lower bound of hit rate (ret>0), in %.
- Universe: current constituents (survivorship bias, not corrected). GOOGL omitted from SP100 list (duplicate of GOOG's earnings events); list is from memory.
